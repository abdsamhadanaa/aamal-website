Industry Transformation with A'amal Group

A'amal Group partners with Weel & Sandvig to bring model-based emission monitoring and energy-efficiency technology to industry in the Kingdom.

Weel & Sandvig's software and digital twins give operators a continuous, accurate view of their emissions and energy use — supporting compliance, reporting and the decisions that cut both emissions and costs.

Our partner Weel & Sandvig

Model-based software and energy-efficient industrial solutions for monitoring and reducing emissions, used by energy majors worldwide. Saudi Aramco and Weel & Sandvig have signed an MoU to explore AI-enabled emission intelligence.
