Our story

Three decades of building, operating and investing

Founded in Al Khobar in 1994 by Yassin Al Suroor, A'amal Group — the Arab Economic & Business Group — has built its reputation on delivering projects on time and within budget, with an unwavering focus on safety, sustainability and quality.

At A'amal, we have a quest for growth — but growing honourably is our code.
