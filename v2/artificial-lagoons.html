Artificial Lagoons

Turn any site into a beach destination — crystalline lagoons of unlimited size, with white sand and year-round swimming.

Overview

Artificial Lagoons with A'amal Group

A'amal Group and Crystal Lagoons have joined forces to bring the world's leading artificial lagoon technology to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Crystal Lagoons' patented technology makes it possible to build crystalline lagoons of unlimited size, surrounded by palm trees and white sandy beaches. Together, we create water destinations for tourism, public events, residential communities and commercial developments.

Our partner Crystal Lagoons

Services

What we offer

Public Access Lagoons (PAL™)

Ticketed beach destinations minutes from home, open to the whole community.

  • New meeting-point destinations that attract up to 1 million visitors a year
  • Suitable for all ages and engaging for the community
  • Revenue from ticketed entry and programmed lagoon activities
  • Turns vacant or under-used sites into profitable destinations
  • Boosts retail foot traffic and the value of surrounding sites
  • Creates substantial local employment

Real Estate Lagoons

A signature amenity that sets a development apart and lifts its value.

  • Mixed-use and town centres: an instant landmark that keeps visitors on site
  • Masterplanned communities: a grand amenity that brings residents together
  • Amenity centres with beach areas, water sports, piers, promenades and parks
  • Accelerates sales, raises property values and supports higher densities

Hospitality & Resorts

Waterfront positioning for hotels anywhere — even far from the sea.

  • Creates first-row, beachfront hotel experiences on inland sites
  • Supports higher room rates, occupancy and food & beverage spend
  • Masterplanning and lagoon activation for resort destinations

Year-round beach life

Crystal Lagoons® Hot Reef technology keeps the water at around 28°C (82°F) at low cost, extending the season into the winter months.

Complete lagoon experiences

With specialist partners, we add the attractions that make a lagoon a full destination.

  • Beach clubs and lifestyle hospitality
  • Floating water parks
  • Artificial surf waves for hotels, resorts and waterparks
