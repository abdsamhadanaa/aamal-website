Services

What we offer

Talent acquisition

Qualified people, from one specialist to a full project team.

  • Contingent labour sourcing — discipline-specific recruiters and a global talent pool
  • Multi-hire campaigns — across a whole project lifecycle or a specific scope
  • Engaged search — direct hire governed by simple service level agreements
  • Master Vendor Program (MVP) — efficient vendor management
  • Workforce Consolidation Program (WCP) — lower contingent labour costs through cohesive management and payroll

Global employment & mobility

We take on the burden of employing and moving people.

  • Global Employment Outsourcing (GEO) — employer of record, benefits, insurance and contracts
  • Global payroll outsourcing — gross-to-net pay, tax filings, payments and equalisation
  • Global immigration — pre-mobilisation, document attestation, visas and work permits
  • Global HR consulting — entity set-up, project management and organisational design
  • Relocation services — policy, origin, destination and localisation support

Managed solutions

Workforce programmes run for you and benchmarked against the market.

  • Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)
  • Contractor and executive compensation benchmarking
  • Managed Service Provision (MSP) for contingent workforces
  • Talent mapping — peer talent pools, live salary data and candidate insight
