Corporate Social Responsibility

A'amal Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

A'amal Group understands the critical need to be an active part of social responsibility projects. Founder & CEO, Mr. Yassin Al Suroor, is an active humanitarian who has been involved in numerous social projects around the world, using his corporate experience to make a difference for a better world. He is Co-founder and Executive Board Member of the National Role Model Society, and a Member of the Board of Trustees of the ICC Merchants of Peace Fund in Paris.
