About Us

Since our inception in 1994, A'amal Group has established an impeccable track record of delivering exceptional projects. We're strategically headquartered in the thriving Eastern Province of Saudi Arabia, hub of superior oil producing, refining and exporting zone of the world.

We're a dynamic, transparent and socially responsible group that is committed to walk an extra mile to fuel the regional economy and improve lives through its various business activities. We understand that each project and client has individual needs and objectives. As such, we're committed to developing individual relationships that enable us to develop the best solution for each and every one of our clients.

At A'amal Group, we stand at the forefront of the 21st century, proudly leading the way with our expertise, innovation, and commitment to excellence in numerous sectors, including Real Estate, Hospitality, Construction Management, Energy Efficiency, Oil and Gas, HR Management, Industry Transformation and Investment.
