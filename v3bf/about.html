About Us

Building a sustainable future

We don’t wait for the future, we help build it. For over 30 years, A’amal Group has driven innovation and progress across the Kingdom and beyond from our headquarters in Al Khobar, in the heart of Saudi Arabia’s oil and gas industry.

With a portfolio spanning Artificial Lagoons - Sports Facilities - Operations & Maintenance - Electrical & Instrumentation - Waterproofing & Industrial Coating - Industry Transformation - Human Capital - Project Management Solution, we are dedicated to enriching the lives of our clients and the communities we serve. Guided by one mission, we grow together.

We believe quality is what builds the future, which is why we are committed to delivering the highest quality projects and services, for today and for generations to come.

Founder & CEO Message

A’amal Group is a dynamic, transparent and socially responsible group that is committed to walk an extra mile to fuel the regional economy and improve lives through our various business activities.

At A’amal, we have a quest for growth but growing honorably is our code. We strive to be a regional leader in all our businesses by accurately identifying and managing business opportunities by establishing strategic alliances through reputable international companies with outstanding track records.

At the same time, we rely on our past experiences and core business principles for strength and courage needed to move forward in this ever changing environment. Today, more than ever we are committed towards building our future through environmentally friendly, state-of-the-art technology and modern management principles.

Last but not least, the strength of A’amal lies in the support of our clients, partners and community. We are ever thankful to them for making us what we are today.

Our purpose

Vision, mission and values

Our vision

We strive to be a regional leader in all our businesses by accurately identifying and managing business opportunities, and by establishing strategic alliances with reputable international companies with outstanding track records.

Our mission

  • Clients: to emphasise quality of service in all our dealings with our clients.
  • Shareholders: to achieve rapid growth and maximise returns for our shareholders.
  • Partners: to form strategic, strong and reliable partnerships.
  • Employees: to maintain a productive work environment that gives our workforce the opportunity to continuously improve their skills and knowledge.
  • Communities: to be a leading regional company that can be trusted by all parties.

Our values

In line with its mission, A'amal Group has adopted a set of core values that are crucial for its success:

  • High moral and ethical standards
  • Commitment to excellence
  • Innovation and initiative
  • Effectiveness and efficiency
  • Development impact
  • A virtuous work environment
