Privacy Policy

How A'amal Group collects, uses and protects the personal information you share through this website.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by A'amal Group (Arab Economic & Business Group), King Abdullah Road, Middle East Commercial Center, Al Khobar 31952, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We are responsible for the personal information described in this notice.

What we collect

  • Information you send us — when you use the contact form or email us: your name, company, email address, phone number and the content of your message.
  • Technical information — like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to keep the site secure and working.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and follow up on business you ask us about.
  • To keep the website secure and fix problems.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run this website and deliver your messages (for example our hosting and form-delivery providers), within A'amal Group companies where needed to answer your enquiry, or where the law requires it. Some of these providers may process data outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia; where they do, we take steps to protect it as required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to deal with them and for our business records, then delete them.

Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law you can ask to know what personal information we hold about you, get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw consent. To make a request, email info@aamal-group.com.

Links to other websites

Our site links to partner websites and to Google Maps. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
