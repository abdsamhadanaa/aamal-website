Sports Facilities with A'amal Group

Our partner Duplays has been designing, building and operating sports venues since 2007, from community leagues to the largest indoor sports venue in the UAE, with projects delivered in Saudi Arabia.

Together we connect players, teams and leagues, and organise the facilities, staff and equipment that make sport happen across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

What we offer

Everything included in our sports facilities service.

01

Design

Expert input on facility design to maximise participation, commercial viability and operational excellence.

02

Sports fit-out

Sports surfaces, fixed equipment and facility layouts from trusted specialist suppliers, matched to your budget.

03

Sports consumables

Balls, bibs, staff and team apparel — sourced from reliable regional vendors.

04

Contracting

Civil works through a preferred contractor list, including dome delivery, MEP works and site development such as parking and landscaping.

Clubhouse design

Event conception & execution

End-to-end sports events, from permits and registration to the final whistle.

  • Event management: venue selection, permits, participant recruitment and registration, scheduling and budgeting
  • Logistics: site set-up and dismantling, branding, team apparel, transport and equipment, medals and trophies, stage, sound and light, seating, toilets and F&B
