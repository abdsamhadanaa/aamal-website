Overview

Waterproofing & Industrial Coating

Turnkey waterproofing, roofing, painting and protective coatings that keep structures and assets sealed, safe and long-lasting.

A'amal Waterproofing & Industrial Coating has earned a reputation for innovative turnkey waterproofing, painting and roofing solutions for industrial, commercial and residential projects.

Industries we serve
  • Industrial plants
  • Oil & gas pipelines
  • Commercial buildings
  • Residential projects
Our services

What we offer

Everything included in our waterproofing & industrial coating service.

01

Waterproofing

Reliable protection across every detail, delivered by a trained, specialised team.

  • Polyurea: hot spray and cold-applied
  • HDPE membranes
  • FPO / TPO / PVC lining
  • Cement-based coatings
  • Hybrid liquid waterproofing membranes
  • Acrylic & PU resin injection
  • Glass Reinforced Plastic (GRP)
02

Thermal insulation

Thermal performance built into the building envelope, to local and international standards.

  • Spray Polyurethane Foam (SPF)
  • Combined thermal + waterproofing systems
  • Insulation boards: XPS / EPS / PIR
  • EIFS exterior insulation & finishing systems
03

Flooring systems

Flooring systems and finishes for a broad range of project requirements.

  • Epoxy and polyurethane
  • Self-leveling and heavy-duty polyurethane
  • Heavy-duty antistatic flooring
  • Microcement & terrazzo
  • Traffic coatings
  • Screed, exposed aggregate and stamped concrete
04

Concrete repair & strengthening

Concrete rehabilitation from crack injection and structural strengthening to surface restoration and leak sealing.

  • Structural crack repair & injection
  • Structural strengthening
  • Expansion joint repair & treatment
  • Leak sealing
  • Repair of honeycombing, voids and casting defects
  • Corrosion protection
  • Industrial chemical-resistant concrete treatment
05

Membrane systems

Sheet membrane systems for durable, watertight protection.

  • HDPE membranes
  • PVC membranes
  • FPO
  • TPO
06

Industrial coatings

Protective coatings for steel, structures and tanks.

  • Alkyd coatings
  • Zinc-rich coatings
  • Bituminous coatings
  • Heat-resistant coatings
  • Intumescent fire-protective coatings
  • Chemical-resistant tank linings
