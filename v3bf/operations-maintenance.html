Overview

Industrial Operations & Maintenance with A'amal Group

Our Industrial Operations & Maintenance company was formed by experts with extensive experience in oil and gas construction, pre-commissioning, commissioning, start-up operations, maintenance, shutdowns and turnarounds across the Middle East.

We support the Kingdom's industry with quality work at every stage of an asset's life — with the health and safety of our people as a constant priority.

Our services

What we offer

Everything included in our industrial operations & maintenance service.

01

Turnkey solutions & construction support

Skilled people and project control for construction and turnkey projects.

  • Management and project control personnel
  • Technical and managerial project supervision
  • Procurement and logistics
  • Multidisciplinary technical specialists
  • Health, safety and environment (HSE) teams
  • Training, learning and development
02

Pre-commissioning

Making sure every system is ready before it is energised.

  • Commissioning testing
  • Risk assessments
  • Validation protocol writing
  • Factory acceptance testing (FAT)
  • Protocol field execution and reports
  • Equipment and system installation inspections
03

Commissioning

Bringing equipment and systems into safe, verified operation.

  • Commissioning plans, procedures and method statements
  • System inspection, testing and debugging
  • Installation of equipment, systems and field devices
  • Calibration and loop check procedures
  • Validation
  • Hydro testing, flushing and chemical cleaning
04

Start-up

A safe, effective start-up and fast troubleshooting.

  • Computer validation
  • Commissioning procedures, inspection and test forms
  • Procedure writing and MCC dossier creation
  • Commissioning and validation planning
  • Commissioning project management
  • Start-up and troubleshooting
05

Maintenance

Reliability programmes that cut downtime and extend asset life.

  • Maintenance and reliability assessments
  • Root cause analysis and training
  • Asset management policy development
  • Turnaround planning and execution
  • Asset hierarchy development
  • Maintenance strategy development
  • Maintenance process mapping and optimisation
  • System and asset criticality
  • Lean manufacturing and total productive maintenance
  • Maintenance programme optimisation and permit to work
