Human Resource Management with A'amal Group

A'amal Group and Airswift come together to provide workforce solutions across talent acquisition, global employment and managed services.

Airswift is an international workforce solutions provider and strategic partner to the energy, process and infrastructure industries. Its turnkey workforce solutions capture and deliver the talent clients need to achieve their goals.

What we offer

Everything included in our human resource management service.

01

Talent acquisition

Specialised technical talent for complex projects, found by discipline-specific recruiters.

  • Professional search & headhunting
  • Contract hiring with pre-vetted specialists
  • Executive & board search

02

Employer of Record

Hire in countries where you have no legal entity, with none of the administrative burden.

  • Payroll, tax and benefits administration

Global mobility

  • Immigration and visa systems
