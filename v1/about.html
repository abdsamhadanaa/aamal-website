About A'amal Group

The Arab Economic & Business Group — a diversified Saudi group building, operating and investing across industries since 1994.

Who we are

Three decades of delivery across industries

Founded in Al Khobar in 1994, A'amal Group has built its reputation on delivering projects on time and within budget, with an unwavering focus on safety, sustainability and quality.

The group works across hospitality and sports, contracting and technical services, energy, and project and business management — from developing facilities to operating and maintaining them, and from managing people to advising businesses.

Headquartered in the Kingdom, A'amal also operates offices in Malaysia and Romania.
