About Us

Three decades of delivery across industries

Founded in Al Khobar in 1994, A'amal Group — the Arab Economic & Business Group — has built its reputation on delivering projects on time and within budget, with an unwavering focus on safety, sustainability and quality.

Our work spans sports facilities, industrial operations, green energy and smart cities, through to human resource management and business consultancy — with a presence in Saudi Arabia, Malaysia and Romania.
